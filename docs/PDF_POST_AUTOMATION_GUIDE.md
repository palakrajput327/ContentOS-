# 📖 Complete User Guide: How to Automate Social Media Posts from a PDF
## ContentOS / SMM Panel — Zero-Touch Autonomous Publishing Engine

---

## 🌟 Overview: What Does This Feature Do?
Yeh feature aapke social media management ko **100% autonomous (Zero-Touch)** bana deta hai:
- Aapko sirf **ek baar apni business PDF** (brochure, whitepaper, catalogue ya report) upload karni hai.
- System khud PDF se saara **text aur images** extract karega.
- AI khud har connected platform (**Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, Telegram, X**) ke liye catchy captions, descriptions aur hashtags generate karega.
- System PDF ke images ko automatically posts ke sath pair karega.
- Posts din-ba-din (**Day 1, Day 2, Day 3...**) sequential time slots me automatically schedule ho jayengi.
- Beech me koi festival aayega toh calendar engine automatically festival greeting post inject kar dega.
- Post live publish hone par aapke **SMM Panel notification bell** aur registered **Gmail** par instant confirmation deliver hoga.

---

## 🚀 3 Simple Steps: How to Use It

### Step 1: Connect Your Social Accounts (One-Time Setup)
1. Left navigation menu se **Social Accounts** / **Settings** par jayein.
2. Apne social channels connect karein:
   - **LinkedIn:** Click "Connect LinkedIn" and grant permissions.
   - **Instagram & Facebook:** Connect via Meta OAuth (select your Facebook page & Instagram Business profile).
   - **Telegram:** Enter your Telegram Bot Token & Channel/Chat ID.
3. Verify karein ki accounts ka status **Connected** (Green) dikhai de raha hai.

---

### Step 2: Upload Your Business PDF
1. Left sidebar se **AI Studio** par click karein.
2. Top tab me se **"PDF Knowledge"** select karein.
3. **"Select PDF File(s)"** button par click karein aur apni PDF choose karein (size up to 50 MB):
   - Chahe company presentation ho, product guide ho, ya research document ho.
4. Upload hote hi background worker kuch hi seconds me:
   - Saara text read karke knowledge chunks bana dega.
   - PDF ke saare embedded images aur visual previews clean thumbnails ke sath extract kar lega.

---

### Step 3: Click "⚡ Start Auto-Pilot" (Hands-Free Mode)
1. Jaise hi PDF upload ho jaye, screen par purple-blue gradient banner dikhega:
   > **⚡ 100% Zero-Touch Auto-Pilot [HANDS-FREE]**
2. Bas **"⚡ Start Auto-Pilot"** button par click kar dijiye!
3. **Bas aapka kaam yahan khatam!** 🎉
   - Software khud sabhi topics ke multi-platform variants banayega.
   - Extracted images ko graphics ke roop me attach karega.
   - Posts ko direct `SCHEDULED` status dekar upcoming calendar days (Day 1 @ 10:00 AM, Day 2 @ 10:00 AM...) par assign kar dega.

---

## 📅 Where to Monitor Your Scheduled Posts?

### 1. In Content Calendar (`/app/calendar`)
- **Calendar** page open karein.
- Aapko aane wale dino ke slots me automated posts sequence-wise scheduled dikhai dengi.
- Beech me agar koi Indian festival (jaise Diwali, Eid, Independence Day) hoga, toh system ne subah ke prime slot me festival poster post auto-inject ki hui hogi.

### 2. In Content Library (`/app/library`)
- Sabhi generated posts `SCHEDULED` badge ke sath list me dikhai dengi.
- Aap chahein toh kisi bhi post par click karke caption ya image preview dekh sakte hain (par koi manual editing compulsory nahi hai).

---

## 🔔 How Notifications Work

Jab bhi background worker scheduled time par post live publish karega ya agar koi platform account reconnect maangta hai:

| Channel | Notification Type | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **SMM Panel Bell Icon** | In-App Real-Time Alert | Bell icon par red badge aayega + post ka live platform link milega. |
| **Registered Gmail** | Direct Email Notification | User ne jis email se signup kiya hai, uske Gmail par `✅ [Published] Your scheduled post is live on LinkedIn` subject ke sath email deliver hoga. |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results
1. **Rich PDFs Work Best:** Aisi PDFs upload karein jisme achha factual text aur images/diagrams hon — AI usme se bohot engaging social posts aur posters nikalta hai.
2. **Posting Slots Customization:** Workspace settings me aap apna preferred daily posting time (e.g. 10:00 AM, 04:00 PM, 08:00 PM) set kar sakte hain.
3. **Multiple PDFs:** Aap ek se zyada PDFs bhi upload kar sakte hain, Auto-Pilot poore workspace ke knowledge pool se content schedule karta rahega.
