# BUILD A PRODUCTION-GRADE AI SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

## Product Name

Use a temporary working name such as **SocialPilot AI / SocialFlow AI / ContentOS**. Keep the branding configurable so the final product name can be changed later.

---

# 1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

Build a premium, production-grade SaaS platform that allows businesses and teams to manage their social-media presence from one workspace.

The platform must allow users to:

1. Create social-media posts manually.
2. Connect multiple social-media accounts.
3. Generate posts, captions and campaigns using AI.
4. Upload PDFs and business documents.
5. Analyze long PDFs and extract business knowledge.
6. Automatically transform document knowledge into social-media content.
7. Generate platform-specific captions and posts.
8. Generate AI-assisted videos in multiple aspect ratios and durations.
9. Preview and edit generated content.
10. Require human approval before publishing when configured.
11. Schedule content.
12. Publish content to connected social channels.
13. Manage multiple team members.
14. Track exactly who created, edited, approved, scheduled and published content.
15. Maintain a complete activity/audit history.
16. Maintain a reusable company Brand Kit and AI knowledge base.

The product should feel like a premium modern B2B SaaS application rather than a basic CRUD dashboard.

Implement RBAC.

Full control.

Admin:

Manage workspace, users, accounts and content.

Content Manager:

Create, edit and schedule content.

Editor:

Create and edit drafts.

Approver:

Viewer:

All backend permissions must be enforced server-side.

Frontend permissions are only for UI visibility and must never be considered security.

# 40. FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

This is not merely a social media scheduler.

The product should be designed as:

**Business Knowledge → AI Content Intelligence → Content Creation → Human Approval → Scheduling → Multi-platform Publishing → Audit → Analytics**

The architecture must remain modular so each part can evolve independently.

Build the foundation cleanly rather than creating a large monolithic component.

Prioritize correctness, security, maintainability, extensibility and premium UX.
